Spectral Bounds of the Generating Graph of $\mathbb{Z}_n$

Authors: Kavita Samant, A. Satyanarayana Reddy

Year of arXiv: 2025

Let $G$ be a group. A group is said to be $k$-generated if it can be generated by its $k$ elements. A generating set of $G$ is called a minimal generating set if no proper subset of it generates G. A minimal generating set of a group can have different sizes. The generating graph $\Gamma(G)$ of a group $G$ is defined as a graph with the vertex set $G$, where two distinct vertices are adjacent if they together generate $G$. This graph is particularly useful when studying 2-generated groups. In this context, consider the group $G=\mathbb{Z}_n$, the integers modulo n. In this paper, we explore various graph-theoretic properties of the generating graph $\Gamma(\mathbb{Z}_n)$ and investigate the spectra of its adjacency and Laplacian matrices. Additionally, we explicitly determine the set of all possible minimal generating sets of $\mathbb{Z}_n$ of size $k$.

Generating Graphs of Finite Dihedral Groups

Authors: Kavita Samant and A. Satyanarayana Reddy

Year of arXiv: 2023
Year of publication: 2023
Volume 78, 7 August 2023, Article no. 200

For a group $G$, the generating graph is defined as the graph with the vertex set $G$, and any two distinct vertices of are adjacent if they generate $G$. In this paper, we study the generating graph of $D_n$ where $D_n$ is a Dihedral group of order $2n$. We explore various graph theoretic properties, and determine complete spectrum of the adjacency and the Laplacian matrix of $\Gamma(D_n)$. Moreover, we compute some distance and degree based topological indices of $\Gamma(D_n)$.

The Generating Graph of Dicyclic Groups.

Authors: Kavita Samant and A. Satyanarayana Reddy

Year of arXiv: 2024
Year of publication: 2026
Volume 74, Issue-4, 11 February 2026, Pages 383-407

For a group $G$, the generating graph of $G,$ denoted by $Γ\Gamma(G)$. We define $Q_n=\langle x,y: x^2=y^4=1,x^n=y^2,y^{-1}xy=x^{-1}\rangle, the dicyclic group of order $4n$. This paper primarily explores the graph characteristics and spectral properties of various matrices associated with $\Gamma(Q_n)$. Specifically, we determine the complete spectrum of the adjacency, Laplacian, distance and eccentricity matrices. Additionally, we completely determine the spectrum of the distance and eccentricity matrices of the dihedral group of order $2n$, denoted as $D_n$.

An Arithmetic Characterization of 2-Generated Numbers

Authors: Kavita Samant, Bireswar Das, and Dhara Thakkar

Year of arXiv: 2025
Year of acceptance: 2026
..

A group $G$ is said to be $k$-generated if it has a generating set with k elements. A positive integer $n$ is called a \emph{2-generated number} if every group of order $n$ is 2-generated. In this article, we establish an arithmetic characterization of 2-generated numbers expressed in terms of the prime factorization of n.
