ABOUT ME

Hello! Welcome to my homepage!

I am Kavita Samant, Currently, I am a visiting researcher at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Gandhinagar, working under the mentorship of Prof. Bireswar Das, where I explore quantum and classical algorithms for problems in computational group theory. I earned my Ph.D. from Shiv Nadar Institution of Eminence, Delhi-NCR worked under the supervision of Dr. A. Satyanarayana Reddy. Before this, I earned my M.Phil. degree from Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University Delhi, under the supervision of Prof. Geetha Venkataraman. I completed my M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Mathematics from University of Delhi, India.

My primary area of interests are Group Theory and Algebraic Graph Theory. In addition to my work, I also have a strong interest in Computational Group Theory and Quantum Computing. My Ph.D. research focused on the generating graphs of finite groups, particularly 2-generated groups, exploring the structural and spectral properties. More details about my research can be found here. I am currently looking for postdoctoral opportunities and always open to research collaborations-feel free to reach out if my work overlaps with your interests.
